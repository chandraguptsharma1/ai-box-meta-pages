User Data Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of eligible data that AI Box collected through the Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business accounts you connected. This page explains how to disconnect an account, how to submit a deletion request, and what happens next.

Never send passwords or access tokens

AI Box will never ask for your Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp password, one-time codes, or access tokens. Do not include them in any request. If someone asks you for them claiming to be AI Box, do not respond.

1.Disconnect an account from AI Box

Disconnecting stops AI Box from retrieving any further data from that account. You can do this in either place:

  1. In AI Box

    {{DISCONNECT_INSTRUCTIONS}}

  2. In your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Business settings

    Open the settings for the account you connected, go to the section that lists business integrations or connected apps, find AI Box and remove it. Menu names may vary between platforms and over time.

Disconnecting does not by itself delete data AI Box has already retrieved. To have that data deleted, submit a deletion request as described below.

2.Submit a deletion request

Send an email to {{DELETION_EMAIL}} with the subject line “AI Box data deletion request”.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your AI Box account where possible. This helps us verify the request faster.

To: {{DELETION_EMAIL}} Subject: AI Box data deletion request Name: [your name] AI Box account email: [email you use with AI Box] Business name: [business, if applicable] Connected account(s): [e.g. Facebook Page name, Instagram @handle, WhatsApp Business account name] Request: Please delete the data AI Box collected through the account(s) above. [Optional] Also delete my AI Box account: yes / no

3.What to include in your request

Please include

  • Your full name
  • The email address used with AI Box
  • The business name, if applicable
  • Which connected account(s) the request covers — Page name, Instagram handle or WhatsApp Business account name
  • Whether you also want your AI Box account deleted

Do not include

  • Passwords for any account
  • Access tokens, API keys or app secrets
  • One-time login or verification codes
  • Payment card or bank details
  • Government ID documents, unless we specifically request them to resolve a dispute about ownership

4.How we verify account ownership

Before deleting data, we need to make sure the request comes from someone entitled to make it. We may:

  • check that the request was sent from the email address registered with your AI Box account, or reply to that address asking you to confirm the request;
  • ask you to confirm details about the connected account that the owner or an administrator would know, such as the Page or account name and approximate date it was connected;
  • where the request is about a business account, confirm that you are an owner or administrator of that business account.

Verification will never require your password, one-time codes or access tokens. If we cannot verify a request, we will tell you and explain what else is needed.

5.Data that may be deleted

Eligible data collected through your connected accounts may include:

Type of dataExamples
Connection records and access tokensThe stored authorization AI Box uses to reach your account
Connected account detailsPage, Instagram or WhatsApp Business account names, IDs and profile details
Retrieved analyticsInsights, engagement metrics and messaging analytics retrieved from the account
Generated reportsAI-generated reports and summaries based on that account's data
AI Box account informationYour name, email and settings — if you ask for your AI Box account to be deleted too

Deleting data from AI Box does not delete anything from Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp themselves. Data on those platforms is managed through their own settings.

6.How requests are processed

  1. We acknowledge your request

    We reply to confirm we have received it, using the email address you wrote from.

  2. We verify the request

    We confirm you are entitled to make the request, as described in section 4.

  3. We revoke access and delete eligible data

    We disconnect the relevant accounts and delete the eligible data from AI Box systems, including data held for us by our service providers.

  4. We confirm completion

    We email you to confirm what was deleted and note anything we were required to keep, with the reason.

We aim to complete verified requests within {{DELETION_RESPONSE_TIME}}. If we need more time, we will let you know why.

7.Information we may need to keep

Where applicable law requires us to, we may retain limited information after a deletion request — for example, records needed to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations, to resolve disputes, or a record that a deletion request was received and completed. Any retained information is kept only for as long as that requirement applies and is not used to provide AI Box features.
