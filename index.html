What AI Box does

Reporting built on data you choose to connect

AI Box only works with business accounts that an authorized user connects, and only with the data those accounts grant permission to access.

Business Analytics

View performance metrics from your connected business accounts in one place, such as reach, engagement and messaging activity where the platform makes them available.

Connected Data Sources

Connect Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and WhatsApp Business accounts using the platform's own login and permission screens. You can disconnect at any time.

AI-Powered Reports

Generate plain-language summaries and reports from your permitted analytics. AI-generated content can contain errors and should be reviewed before you rely on it.

How it works

Permission first, at every step

1. You authorize

An authorized user signs in through the platform's official login flow and reviews the permissions AI Box requests. AI Box never asks for your social media password.

2. We retrieve permitted data

AI Box retrieves only the analytics and account information covered by the permissions you granted, for the purpose of producing your reports.

3. You get a report

AI Box assembles the data and uses AI to help produce a report. You remain in control and can disconnect accounts or request deletion.
