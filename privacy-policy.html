2.Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, AI Box may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — details you provide when you register for or use AI Box, such as your name, business name and email address.
  • Connected account information — identifiers and profile details of the business accounts you choose to connect (for example, a Facebook Page name and ID). See sections 3 to 5.
  • Analytics and business data — metrics and insights made available by connected platforms under the permissions you grant, and shipment or logistics data that you or your organization authorize AI Box to access.
  • Reports and inputs — reports AI Box generates for you and any instructions or questions you enter when requesting a report.
  • Technical and usage information — information needed to operate the service, such as log records of sign-in and connection events, and basic device and browser information.
  • Communications — messages you send to us, for example support or deletion requests.

AI Box does not ask for, and you should never send us, your Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp password. Platform connections are made through the platform's own login and permission screens.

3.Connected business account data

AI Box only accesses a Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business account after an authorized user connects it and grants permission. When you connect an account:

  • the platform shows you which permissions AI Box is requesting, and you can choose whether to continue;
  • the platform issues AI Box an access credential (an access token) that allows AI Box to request only the data covered by the permissions you granted;
  • AI Box uses that access to retrieve data needed to produce your analytics and reports.

You must only connect accounts that you own or are authorized to manage on behalf of a business. You can review or remove AI Box's access at any time — see Account disconnection.

10.Your choices

  • Choose what to connect. You decide which accounts to connect and can decline permissions on the platform's consent screen. Declining some permissions may limit the reports AI Box can produce.
  • Disconnect. You can remove AI Box's access to a connected account at any time.
  • Access and correction. You can ask us for a copy of personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete information collected through your connected accounts.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law. To exercise any of these choices, contact us at {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

11.Account disconnection

You can stop AI Box from accessing a connected account in either of these ways:

  • In AI Box: {{DISCONNECT_INSTRUCTIONS}}
  • On the platform: remove AI Box from the business integrations or connected apps section of your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Business settings. Menu names may vary.

Disconnecting stops future data retrieval. It does not by itself delete data AI Box has already retrieved — to request that, follow the Data Deletion Instructions.
